title: "Relevance and Influence "
date: 2026-07-21T10:15:40.195Z
---
Had a session with my mentoring circle today and some revolving themes that came up as we discussed career goals and expectations of the mentoring session were the desire to grow in knowledge and competency as well as to find purpose and meaning in what we do.\
\
I kind of boiled it down to 2 words - [hashtag#Relevance](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23relevance&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED) & [hashtag#Influence](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23influence&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED)\
\
When we talk about the desire to grow in knowledge and competency, what exactly is driving that urge to grow and acquire more subject matter expertise or to expand our knowledge and understanding of things around us? I personally believe a huge part of it is our need to feel relevant to the people, environment, organisation etc that we are part of. I can't imagine how I will be coping if one day I wake up to find myself totally irrelevant to society and people around me. One professor at [hashtag#INSEAD](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23insead&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED) once told me that every organism that exists has to serve a purpose, otherwise it's only course is to shrivel up and die. And until this day I strongly feel that having a sense of purpose really gives me the reason to push beyond my comfort zone and continue to grow and be a better version of myself so that I can continue to be relevant to the world I live in, the organisations I serve and the people I touch. To have zero relevance to the world around me would leave me feeling totally hollowed out and empty.\
\
Which brings me to the point about Influence. To me, different life stages bring about it different challenges and as we progress in our life and career, we will undoubtedly exert Influence on the people and circumstances around us. Influence is a huge part of [hashtag#leadership](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23leadership&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED) and [hashtag#management](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23management&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED). The extent of Influence we have in a way defines how impactful a leader we are and precisely because of this, the more Influence we can exert, the more careful we have to be in understanding the consequences of our decisions and actions. So for those of us who are making our way up the career ladder or already perched somewhere near the top never forget the [hashtag#Duty_Of_Care](https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23duty_of_care&origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED) we owe to the people around us. As Uncle Ben once taught me, "With great power comes great responsibility"\
\
